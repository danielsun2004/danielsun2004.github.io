Daniel Sun

MSc Student, Department of Statistical Sciences
University of Toronto

I'm an MSc Statistics student at the University of Toronto supervised by Dr. Lisa Strug, doing research on machine learning.

My research interests revolve heavily around statistical principles of information transfer between learned models. Specifically how model uncertainty, disagreement, dependence and distribution shift affect teacher-student, ensemble model and multi-model learning. I'm looking for research opportunities working on teacher → student, peer ↔ peer, federated learning/distillation and transfer learning.

I'm currently a Machine Learning Researcher at Strug Lab, implementing and benchmarking machine learning methods for binding and potency prediction. I also develop Machine Learning and Data Science modules under the supervision of Dr. Gracia Dong.

Outside of research, I am a member of the Statistical Society of Canada and moonlight as a derivatives trader using ML. I also enjoy hiking and listening to comedy.
